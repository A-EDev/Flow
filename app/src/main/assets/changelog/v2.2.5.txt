NEW FEATURES
- Video search now runs on the native InnerTube client: one request per page instead of thirteen, with filters and sorting applied by the server
- Search rebuilt: a Material 3 search bar, filters in a sheet, a grid/list toggle, and creator cards tinted by the channel avatar with their latest uploads attached
- Channels you block are now hidden from search results, not just the feed
- Channel pages moved to the native InnerTube client, with correct scheduled-stream and premiere handling

- Explore rebuilt around YouTube's own destinations — Live, Gaming, Music, Movies, News, Sports, Learning and Fashion — each rendered in the shape its source serves, with the content region one tap away in the top bar

- The Flow Control Center is now Your taste, rebuilt on the settings layout with your video and music taste, engine details, a Hidden from you page for everything you blocked, and profiles that sync between devices and export to a file
- Artist pages show your own history with that artist: play count, liked status, most-played tracks, and which "fans also like" artists are already in your rotation

- The recommendation brain now learns from real watch time, early skips, downloads, Watch Later, playlist saves, live subscribes and the searches you type, and learns what each channel is about from its creator tags and recent uploads

PERFORMANCE
- Opening an artist, album or Daily Mix page no longer reloads the entire music home feed behind the scenes — one shared engine serves every music page, cutting Daily Mix opens from up to 30 seconds to about a second and ending a major source of battery drain and heat
- Nothing animates behind a hidden layer any more: the seek bar wave, lyrics sync, mini player equalizer, queue waveform and lyrics loading indicator all pause when their surface is not visible, ending a constant per-frame drain across entire listening sessions
- Music shelves only recompose while the Music screen is visible, similar-song lanes are cached, and each track change fetches its similar-tracks lane once instead of twice
- Feed ranking noise scales with your actual scores instead of drowning them when the feed was stale
- Flow opens as soon as its first screen is ready instead of holding every launch behind a two second splash, and on Android 13 and later the splash matches your theme and app icon
- Loading placeholders now take the exact shape of the cards they stand in for, so the feed no longer jumps when it arrives, and they no longer redraw the whole layout on every frame
- A YouTube playlist is fetched once instead of twice in full on every open, Local media no longer rescans the device on every visit, and download progress updates once a second instead of four times
- The expanded music player is no longer rebuilt on every song change, and lyrics stop updating while the music is paused
- Feed ranking builds its lookups once per pass instead of once per video, impressions are recorded off the main thread, and scroll and rotation bookkeeping is saved to disk less often
- Opening and collapsing the video player no longer jumps ahead after a slow frame, collapsing glides into the corner without a bounce, and a swipe's speed carries into the motion
