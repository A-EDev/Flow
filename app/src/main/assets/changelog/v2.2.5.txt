FLOW CHANGE LOG
VERSION: 2.2.5
DATE: 2026-09-15
STATUS: PRE-RELEASE

NEW FEATURES
- Video search now runs on the native InnerTube client: one request per page instead of thirteen, with filters and sorting applied by the server
- Search rebuilt: a Material 3 search bar, filters in a sheet, a grid/list toggle, and creator cards tinted by the channel avatar with their latest uploads attached
- Channels you block are now hidden from search results, not just the feed
- Channel pages moved to the native InnerTube client, with correct scheduled-stream and premiere handling
- Private notes on any channel or video, included in backup and device sync
- The video player is now adaptive: a supporting pane and navigation rail on wide screens, with the description, chapters, comments and settings served in the pane or a fullscreen drawer
- Player comments and descriptions now come from InnerTube, and every player surface moves to Material 3 Expressive
- Portrait fullscreen is rebuilt around the video with the title and channel above it, reached the same way by swipe or by the fullscreen button, and the volume and brightness read-outs can now be circular, vertical, a bar, or just the number
- One grid layout across Home, Channels and Search: your cards-per-row setting applies everywhere, and rows the grid cannot fill switch to full-width list cards on tablets and foldables
- A local music taste engine ranks every music shelf and composes Quick Picks, learning the artists you love from how much of each song you actually play — entirely on-device
- New shelves built from your taste and rendered with no network calls: On Repeat, Rediscover for loved artists you have not played in weeks, a time-of-day rotation, and "From artists you love" for their latest releases
- Daily Mixes cluster the artists you listen to together and open as full playlist pages you can play, shuffle and save
- Endless music radio: similar songs are appended as the queue runs low, and the up-next list stays stable instead of changing every song
- Tell the music engine what you don't want: "Not interested" softens an artist for two weeks, "Don't recommend" blocks them everywhere, both reversible
- The Flow Control Center now shows your music taste — top artists, listening rhythm, discovery appetite, genres and blocked-artist management — and the whole profile syncs between devices and exports to a file
- Artist pages show your own history with that artist: play count, liked status, most-played tracks, and which "fans also like" artists are already in your rotation
- New On Repeat home-screen widget puts the songs you keep replaying one tap away
- The engine learns genres and moods from what you play, and keeps a private monthly listening ledger on your device — minutes, plays, discoveries and your listening clock — which never leaves the device
- Full music player redesign: a pill mini player with a progress ring, a lyrics sheet with source browsing, editing, alignment and sync-offset controls, and a rebuilt queue with drag-to-reorder, swipe actions and an endless-radio toggle
- Album colours flow through the entire player with guaranteed readability on any cover, alongside six Material 3 expressive seek bar styles and an Immersive Artwork background that follows your swipes
- The whole music section moves to Material 3 Expressive: wide carousels, grouped Quick Picks, cookie-shaped artists, tonal mood tiles, artwork-tinted pages, and redesigned recognition screens whose mic morphs while it listens
- Home feed serves every one of your interests: your biggest are always present and smaller ones rotate through the remaining slots, and each load descends one level deeper into every interest instead of repeating the surface
- The feed never dead-ends: when the usual sources thin out, loading more digs fresh related-video lanes seeded from what is on screen, your history, likes and playlists
- Fresh subscription uploads are drawn from your whole subscription feed rather than the handful fetched this refresh
- The recommendation brain now learns from real watch time, early skips, downloads, Watch Later, playlist saves, live subscribes and the searches you type, and learns what each channel is about from its creator tags and recent uploads
- Device pairing can now be completed by entering connection details by hand when a QR scan is not practical

IMPROVEMENTS
- Home, Subscriptions, Library and the video player were restructured into shared, reusable components, and the feed pipeline was rebuilt to fix pagination stalls
- Music content follows your chosen region instead of the device locale, and music home caches invalidate on a region change so shelves refresh immediately
- Every music shelf — Speed dial, Listen again, Recommended, music videos and long listens — is now ranked by your taste rather than arriving in YouTube's order, and the music page leads with charts and moods while the engine is new, your own taste once it matures
- Stop showing the same videos over and over: something you actually saw is filtered out of the next refreshes and returns gently later, cached reserve videos are consumed once served, and pull-to-refresh builds a genuinely fresh page
- Related videos rotate their seeds and spread one per interest, and search queries whose results you have mostly seen are skipped
- Blocking a topic is now precise: it no longer blocks every topic in the same category, so blocking "art" no longer hides "startup"
- Channels you interact with are no longer scored below channels you have never seen, and only a couple of fresh subscription uploads pin to the top, one per channel
- A one-time brain revitalization recovers your real interests from your channel knowledge, repairing profiles hollowed out by old learning bugs
- Music radio no longer plays the same artist twice in a row, including across the seam where new tracks are appended
- Hiding an artist now hides their albums too: a track or album is hidden when any credited artist is hidden, so collaborations no longer slip through
- Google Takeout imports now work with localized ZIP archives

PERFORMANCE
- Opening an artist, album or Daily Mix page no longer reloads the entire music home feed behind the scenes — one shared engine serves every music page, cutting Daily Mix opens from up to 30 seconds to about a second and ending a major source of battery drain and heat
- Nothing animates behind a hidden layer any more: the seek bar wave, lyrics sync, mini player equalizer, queue waveform and lyrics loading indicator all pause when their surface is not visible, ending a constant per-frame drain across entire listening sessions
- Music shelves only recompose while the Music screen is visible, similar-song lanes are cached, and each track change fetches its similar-tracks lane once instead of twice
- Feed ranking noise scales with your actual scores instead of drowning them when the feed was stale

FIXES AND STABILITY
- Fix all related-music fetching silently broken by a YouTube layout change, which had starved similar-music sections and flooded Quick Picks with regional charts
- Fix the music feed crashing after an update: release builds could mis-read the artist lists stored inside history, queues, playlists and downloads — stored tracks are now repaired on read, with no clean install needed
- Fix custom themes losing their colours in release builds
- Fix listened songs never reaching the music engine when playback was stopped, the queue replaced, or the track shipped without a duration
- Fix watch progress on normal videos never reaching your recommendation brain, so it learned almost nothing from what you actually watched
- Fix a watched video losing its recorded length when reopened, which silently erased its progress bar
- Fix topic-pair learning deleting every learned connection the moment it was created, and mature brains being unable to pick up new interests no matter how much you watched them
- Fix videos dated "3 days ago" being treated as seconds old, and related videos being stamped with the current time, which let stale uploads crowd out genuinely new ones
- Fix junk words like "right" leaking into your learned interests, and a liked video failing to teach the brain when its details could not load
- Fix the lyrics sync offset having no effect: it now shifts line highlighting, scrolling and word-by-word fills live, and the control got an expressive redesign
- Fix a playback-statistics crash on some devices by replacing Media3's tracker with a wall-clock session tracker
- Fix the ambient player glow and cap session artwork at the platform limit, ending a crash on some ROMs
- Fix a tablet held upright splitting the player into two narrow columns, and the volume gesture starting from a stale level or dropping a boost above 100%

BUILD AND CI
- Add offline recommendation and music benchmarks with regression floors, so feed diversity and repetition are verified by tests instead of claims
- Add offline brain diagnostics that inspect a real exported profile through the production clustering and discovery code
- Search, channel and player parsing are now covered by tests against real captured API responses
- Limit concurrent Kotlin compilations so CI stops running out of memory
